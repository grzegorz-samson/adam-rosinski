Sound research / recording / new technologies

Adam Rosiński

Research in listening, recording, spatial audio, and contemporary sound technologies.

Research into sound as a perceptual, spatial, and technological phenomenon is connected here with recording practice, the design of listening conditions, and music-oriented teaching.
