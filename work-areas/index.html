From research to prototype

A practical chain from listening theory to custom systems.

  1. Listening research Auditory perception, stream segregation, and the way listeners organise complex sound scenes.
  2. Spatial model Translating perception into spatial relations, directionality, and capture strategies.
  3. Prototype design Building physical systems that expose microphone placement, movement, and spatial parameters as creative variables.
  4. Recording scenario Testing the device in studio, performance, installation, and field-recording contexts.
  5. Artistic research use Using the system as a compositional interface and as a laboratory for new listening situations.
